# Journal

Append-only, UTC. One line per event, at most 200 characters, ordinary words and spaces:
`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM TAG text`. Log what happened, never what you didn't do. A search round is one FIND
line (sources, adverts read, queued). Skips and other detail belong in tracker notes, not here.
Tags: SETUP, MAIL, FIND, SUBMIT, FAIL, ASK, OWNER (any owner message), NEXT (a next stage), REPLY, OFFER, SUMMARY, DECISION.
Example: `2026-10-20 09:14 SUBMIT A12 Acme Customer Support Advisor, greenhouse, success page evidence/A12.png`
Example: `2026-10-21 08:02 NEXT A12 Acme video interview due 2026-10-24 17:00 BST, next-steps/A12-acme.md`
