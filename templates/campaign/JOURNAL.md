# Journal

Append-only, UTC. One line per event: `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM TAG text`.
Tags: SETUP, MAIL, FIND, APPLY, SUBMIT, FAIL, SKIP, ASK, ANSWER, NOTIFY, ACCOUNT, BROWSER, OFFER, REPLY, DECISION.
Example: `2026-10-20 09:14 SUBMIT A12 Acme Customer Support Advisor, Greenhouse, success page evidence/A12.png`
