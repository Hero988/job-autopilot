# job-autopilot

Instructions and helper scripts for an AI agent that runs a job search: it finds jobs, applies,
tracks applications and manages a mailbox until a job offer arrives. A campaign's method is its own
`AGENTS.md` (copied from `templates/campaign/AGENTS.md`); `guides/` are reference. During a campaign
this repo is read-only at `.autopilot/`; all state lives in the campaign folder.
